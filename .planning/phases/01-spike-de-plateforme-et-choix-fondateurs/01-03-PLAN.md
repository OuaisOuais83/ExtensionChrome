## Edge Assumptions (no SPEC.md for this phase)

- One production host only. The connector URL is `https://{host}/mcp`; every route (OAuth, /authorize, /securite, spike pages) lives on that host. No redirect is configured between apex and subdomain.
- If the domain zone is not yet active when deploying, the executor deploys on workers.dev with PUBLIC_BASE_URL set to that URL, records « domaine en attente » in the decision journal, and the phase cannot close PLAT-02 until the domain is wired (the verifier will report it).
- A second run must be idempotent: existing `relay-db`, `relay-content` and `OAUTH_KV` are reused only after their jurisdiction is confirmed; a D1 database found outside the EU is deleted and recreated before any data is written (it holds only spike probes).
- SPIKE_ACCESS_CODE is piped to `wrangler secret put` from the environment and never echoed, written to a file, or committed.
