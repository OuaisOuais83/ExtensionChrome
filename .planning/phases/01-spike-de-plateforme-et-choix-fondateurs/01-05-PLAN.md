## Edge Assumptions (no SPEC.md for this phase)

- Corpus size is counted in Unicode code points (what a person calls "characters"); UTF-16 length and UTF-8 bytes are reported alongside because emoji count twice in UTF-16.
- A `[[jalon i/N]]` marker sits at the start of every 1 000-code-point block so the owner can see where a truncated argument stops without the tool echoing content.
- `measure_text` accepts at most 400 000 characters (zod max); larger input is refused by schema validation, and the SDK refuses bodies over 4 MiB.
- The corpora are not committed as files: `/spike/corpus/:size` serves them and `expected.json` pins their measurements; `generate-fixtures.ts --check` detects any drift of the generator.
